# Kiku: screens every design must deliver

Kiku is the voice-agent console for The Brenlow, a fictional hotel in Edinburgh. Kiku (the AI voice agent) answers the hotel line and places outbound calls. Human staff watch calls, join to listen, and take over when a call is escalated. When a specialist takes over, **karaoke mode** runs: Kiku keeps writing the line it would say, the specialist sees that script, and speaks, often improvising. Afterwards the audit shows both **what Kiku wrote** and **what the specialist said**.

All content comes from `designs/_shared/data.js` (`window.KIKU`). Load it with `<script src="../../_shared/data.js"></script>`. Don't invent features, integrations, metrics or claims beyond it. You may write UI copy: labels, buttons, helper text, empty or error text.

Frame: 1440×900 desktop viewport, captured at 2x. Every page fits the viewport exactly (`html, body { height: 100%; overflow: hidden }`). Long lists and transcripts scroll inside their own pane and should end cleanly at the fold.

Roles:
- **Specialist** (Priya Raman): views call logs, joins live calls, takes over escalated calls. Pages 02–06 are her session. Her nav has no Users item.
- **Admin** (Isla Ferguson): creates users, views call logs. Pages 07–10 are her session. Her nav includes Users.
- Outbound call setup appears in both navs. Which role owns it is still an open product question.

Status vocabulary, exact labels: **Completed · In progress · Escalated · Failed**. Each status must be readable without colour, so pair every colour with a shape or icon and the label.
Direction: **Inbound** (someone rang the hotel line +44 131 496 0400) or **Outbound**. Outbound types: **Campaign · Upsell · Make a call**.
